01 / About

Finance depth, built for consulting

I'm an MBA candidate at Cornell Johnson and a Forté Fellow, pursuing a career in consulting. Before Johnson, I spent more than ten years in finance in Brazil, from M&A due diligence at EY and audit to financial reporting and finance leadership at XP Inc., Bitso and Travelier.

I do my best work when a business is in transition: rebuilding cash and controls, delivering a company's first external audit, and keeping a team steady through a restructuring.

    World's largest travel-tech group for ground and sea transportation; 5 global brands, 120+ countries, 14M monthly visitors

    2020 – 2023

    One of Brazil's largest financial institutions; a Nasdaq public company; R$2T+ AUM, 60+ legal entities
